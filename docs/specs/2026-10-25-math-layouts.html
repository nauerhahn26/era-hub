DESIGN PREVIEW · TOUCH DEMO · NO GAZE RUNTIME

Math exploration for Ellie

Main proposal: choose a number, inspect its quantity, and explore what changes. A quiet white workspace holds the math; a few stable edge controls do the changing. These four sample views are for design review.

Reviewer controls — outside the child screen 1920 × 1080 geometry, scaled to fit

Touch a control to explore the proposal.

Shared behavior: math objects are passive. Count highlights the existing collection; it never adds objects. Back opens the number view from partners or ten and ones. The illustrated doors use the shared geometry: Exit at the top left, Talk at the top center, in a 9% high bar. The unlabeled black bottom-right corner is always inert.

Review boundary: the tabs load named samples; they are not child controls. The representation selector keeps the current quantity. The picker remembers the previous workspace, includes zero, and keeps each last digit in the same seat across ranges. This demo stops at 50 for review, with no unlock or implied learning ceiling. Choosing a numeral is not counted as evidence of mathematical understanding.

Picker tradeoff to review: Back stays at the top left. Ten numerals plus Back and Other numbers make 12 body targets, or 14 including the two doors. Other numbers opens six range choices and Back, or 9 targets including the doors. Number partners is limited to 0–10 in this first representation; its range view offers 0–9 and 10. Larger-number exploration remains available in Numbers and Ten and ones. The extra range-selection step reduces navigation commands. Lower-density layouts may be considered after a gaze trial.

Count demonstration: one touch starts a visual model through the counters, then leaves the quantity intact. Spoken counting and descriptions are proposed behavior; this offline review does not play audio. Number partners has one countable inventory split between two trays; the whole is a numeral, not another collection. Moving one counter preserves the whole. Empty endpoints stay calmly inactive in their original seats.
