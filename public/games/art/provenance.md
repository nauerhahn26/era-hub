# Fashion Studio artwork

`studio.png`, `runway.png` and `garden.png` are original backgrounds made for this app on
2026-10-25 using the built-in image tool. They have no third-party character,
photograph or game-art references. All were inspected for empty central
staging space and the absence of baked-in controls or text. Garments and
characters are editable original vector layers in `character.svg` and the
renderer, created for this app without copied third-party sprites.

## Studio prompt

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: original background art for a local children's fashion-design game, landscape 16:9.
Primary request: a polished illustrated 2D fashion design studio with warm daylight, fabric rolls and neatly arranged sewing supplies along the edges, a large full-length mirror, small plants, soft lilac and mint accents, friendly stylish atmosphere for ages six to eight. The central area is open and uncluttered so an animated character can be overlaid later.
Style/medium: premium hand-painted storybook game illustration, crisp expressive shapes with subtle texture and gentle dimensional shading. Sophisticated playful art, not toddler clipart or a wireframe.
Composition: straight-on view, open pale neutral center and clear floor, illustrated worktable and racks frame the sides, no people, no garments floating in the center.
Constraints: Background only. No UI, buttons, lettering, logos, watermarks or text. No identifiable real people or copyrighted characters.

## Runway prompt

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: original landscape 16:9 background art for a children's fashion runway game.
Primary request: a polished illustrated 2D mini fashion-show venue with a broad empty stage floor leading to a warm ivory backdrop, an elegant curved arch, layered lilac curtains, mint and warm peach highlights, softly glowing stage lights and small clusters of flowers at the side edges. Premium playful game art for children six to eight; a show they proudly direct.
Composition/framing: front-facing scene with central 60 percent of image left open for a full-body illustrated character to be layered later; unobstructed neutral center, decorative details at outer edges. A shallow perspective floor. No audience heads at bottom and no runway extending into the foreground.
Style/medium: crisp hand-painted 2D storybook illustration with delicate texture and soft dimensional shading, cohesive warm pastel palette, sophisticated rather than toddler clipart.
Constraints: background only, no characters, no UI, buttons, lettering, logos, watermarks or text.

## Garden prompt

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: original landscape 16:9 background for a children's fashion-design game's garden show venue.
Primary request: a polished illustrated 2D outdoor garden stage on a sunny gentle afternoon, flowering greenery and small trees framing the sides, cream stone terrace in the foreground, an open central lawn and soft blue sky behind. A delicate flower arch sits in the background; the central floor and center view are open for a full-body fashion character overlaid later.
Style/medium: premium hand-painted storybook game illustration, crisp playful shapes, delicate texture, dimensional gentle shading, warm mint/lilac/peach accents matching a stylish fashion studio. For six- to eight-year-olds.
Composition: front-facing, clear empty center occupying the middle60percent, decorative plants mostly toward the edges, unobstructed terrace floor, shallow perspective. No audience, people, statues, mannequins or characters.
Constraints: background only, no text, UI, buttons, lettering, logos or watermark.
