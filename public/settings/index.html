🎙 Voice

The voice that talks to your child in Making Words and The Pencil. Tap any voice to hear it and choose it.

⏱ Dwell time

How long she holds her gaze on a button before it activates. Longer = calmer, fewer accidental picks. Shorter = faster once she’s confident.

1200 ms

🗺 Page-change pause

After a page changes, gaze buttons wait this long before they can start charging — so a fresh page never grabs the tile she happens to be looking at while she reorients. TD Snap on an I-13 has its own copy of this setting (“Delay after page change”, set to 0.25 s).

0.25 s

👁 Cursor feel

How the gaze cursor moves and settles. If it feels jumpy, choose Calmer. If it feels laggy, choose Snappier.

Adjusts smoothing and how firmly the cursor locks onto what she’s looking at.

🙈 Look-away forgiveness

How long she can glance away mid-selection without losing her progress. Rett girls often look away right as they choose — more forgiveness helps.

🖱 Cursor visible?

Show the soft ring where she’s looking. On is best for most activities.

🔊 Volume cap

Keeps the speaker from going louder than the cap — Ellie can still turn it down or mute. It starts by itself at boot and heals itself if it dies (back within 30 minutes). This switch is for pushing it back on right now, or pausing it briefly for testing.

🎵 Music volume cap

How loud the Music board is allowed to play, as a share of the speaker’s volume. One knob for every device — set low for the classroom. Speech is not affected.

100%

⏸ Pause eye-gaze (testing)

Temporarily stop ERAgaze from moving the cursor, so you can check whether another program behaves differently without it. The eye-gaze keeps running in the background and turns back on the moment you tap Running.
