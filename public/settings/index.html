🧩 Apps

Which apps this computer offers. Untick to hide an app and remove its shortcut; tick to add it. Same choices as at install.

⬆️ Updates

New ERA looks for a newer version by itself — shortly after it starts and every 6 hours after that — and installs it quietly. Press the button to look right now instead of waiting.

🎙 Voice

The voice that talks to your child in Making Words and The Pencil. Tap any voice to hear it and choose it.

Premium voices need a free ElevenLabs account. Without a key the built-in computer voice is used.

✏️ Her writing from The Pencil

When your child taps Send in The Pencil, her words are saved here — and emailed to you if you set that up. Email needs a free Resend account (no card). Its free sender only delivers to the address you sign up with, so use that one.

🔗 Add content with Google Drive

Put things in your Google Drive and they appear here by themselves: photos of her clothes in a clothing folder, book packages in books, songs in music, lesson files in content. Checked every 10 minutes (every 6 hours without the Drive app).

🤖 AI helper

An AI key lets New ERA prepare content for your child — clothing photos get trimmed, straightened, named and paired into daily outfits automatically. Pick the AI you already use; the key stays on this computer and is only used for your own photos.

Why a key and not a login? The AI companies don’t allow signing in with your Claude or ChatGPT account inside apps like New ERA — your own key is their official way. Getting one takes about a minute, and Google’s is free (no card needed).

Note: Google’s free AI Studio tier may use what is sent to it — her clothing photos, the words on her book pages — to improve Google’s products; that is in Google’s terms. A pay-as-you-go Google key is not used that way, and neither are the other providers.

📚 Your books

Photograph the pages of one of her picture books, make a folder inside books in your Google Drive named after the book, and put the photos in it. New ERA reads the words off the photos, records the narration and the book turns up in Book Reader on every device in the family — you only pay for it once. Nothing ever leaves your own Drive.

Books read best with a free Google AI Studio key — two free readers check every page, and the pages they disagree on are the ones marked for you to glance at. With only that free key: the words, a few pages a day, and the reader turns the pages silently. Add an ElevenLabs voice key and she hears the book read to her, for a few cents. Recommended: the free Google key plus ElevenLabs — every page read twice, the whole book read aloud, still only cents. A paid AI key (Claude, ChatGPT, or Google’s paid tier) buys speed rather than words: a whole book the same day instead of a few pages a day. Nothing is locked: every setup builds books, the cheaper ones just take longer or stay silent.

🚪 Where the door goes

Every app has a door in its corner. Choose what she lands on when she looks at it.

⏱ Dwell time

How long she holds her gaze on a button before it activates. Longer = calmer, fewer accidental picks. Shorter = faster once she’s confident.

1200 ms

🗺 Page-change pause

After a page changes, gaze buttons wait this long before they can start charging — so a fresh page never grabs the tile she happens to be looking at while she reorients. TD Snap on an I-13 has its own copy of this setting (“Delay after page change”, set to 0.25 s).

0.25 s

👁 Cursor feel

How the gaze cursor moves and settles. If it feels jumpy, choose Calmer. If it feels laggy, choose Snappier.

Adjusts smoothing and how firmly the cursor locks onto what she’s looking at.

🙈 Look-away forgiveness

How long she can glance away mid-selection without losing her progress. Rett girls often look away right as they choose — more forgiveness helps.

🖱 Cursor visible?

Show the soft ring where she’s looking. On is best for most activities.

🔊 Volume cap

Keeps the speaker from going louder than the cap — Ellie can still turn it down or mute. It starts by itself at boot and heals itself if it dies (back within 30 minutes). This switch is for pushing it back on right now, or pausing it briefly for testing.

🎵 Music volume cap

How loud the Music board is allowed to play, as a share of the speaker’s volume. One knob for every device — an open Music board follows within half a minute. Speech is not affected.

100%

⏸ Pause eye-gaze (testing)

Temporarily stop ERAgaze from moving the cursor, so you can check whether another program behaves differently without it. The eye-gaze keeps running in the background and turns back on the moment you tap Running.
