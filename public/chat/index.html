      Turn timings
      
Only timing numbers and outcome categories are saved on this browser, for up to 40 recent turns. No questions, replies, audio or identities are included. Clear them at any time.

      
Release to transcript includes audio draining and recognition. Replies ready to revealed measures the reveal itself; a sealed batch shows at once. Selection to playback measures the wait until the actual voice start event, including any voice preparation. Worker final time combines activity detection and recognition unless separate measurements are available.

      
Medians need three completed measurements in the same turn category; p95 needs twenty. Cancelled and failed turns are counted separately. Missing timings are left out.
